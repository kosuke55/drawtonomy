---
id: REQ-AEB
title: Autonomous emergency braking (AEB)
---
When a collision with a target ahead in the ego lane is imminent (moving, braking, stopped, or
cutting in), the AEB shall brake without driver input and avoid the collision. Detection and
braking shall work at the same level for cars and motorcycles.

Children:

- REQ-AEB-001 Braking lead vehicle
- REQ-AEB-002 Stationary target
- REQ-AEB-003 Cut-in vehicle

References: UN R152, Euro NCAP AEB Car-to-Car and Car-to-Motorcyclist.
