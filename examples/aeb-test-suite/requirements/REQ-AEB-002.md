---
id: REQ-AEB-002
title: Stationary target
parent: REQ-AEB
---
When the ego approaches a stopped target in its lane, the AEB shall not hit it. On low-friction
roads it shall not hit it whenever ideal braking could stop in time.

- Ego speed: 20-100 km/h
- Road friction: 0.3-1.0
- Verified by: TC-AEB-002 (Euro NCAP CCRs, car), TC-AEB-002-MC (motorcycle)
