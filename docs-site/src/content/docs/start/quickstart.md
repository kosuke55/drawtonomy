Five minutes from a blank canvas to an exported scene. No install.

## 1. Open the app

Go to [drawtonomy.com](https://drawtonomy.com). Everything runs in
the browser; nothing is uploaded.

## 2. Draw a lane

1. Press <kbd>N</kbd>, or click the **Lane** tool in the toolbar.
2. Click on the canvas to place the start of the centerline, click
   again for the next vertex, and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to finish.
3. The left and right boundaries appear automatically.

:::tip
Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> while drawing to bypass [snap](/guides/snap/)
for that click.
:::

## 3. Place a vehicle

1. Press <kbd>P</kbd> to open the **Participants** tool.
2. Pick **Vehicle → Sedan** and click on the lane you just drew.
3. Drag a corner handle to resize, or use the rotation handle to
   align it with the lane.

## 4. Export the scene

Open the export menu and pick a format:

- **PNG / SVG / PDF** — share as a flat image.
- **drawtonomy.svg** — saves every connection so you (or a teammate)
  can re-open and keep editing later.
- **Export for esmini** — a zip with OpenDRIVE + OpenSCENARIO ready
  to play in [esmini](https://github.com/esmini/esmini).

[Export your scene](/guides/export/) covers the formats in detail.
