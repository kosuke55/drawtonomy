drawtonomy is a browser-based whiteboard for driving diagrams.
You place lanes, vehicles, pedestrians, and traffic elements on an infinite
canvas, and export the result for use in simulators, planning documents,
or teaching material.

The app lives at [drawtonomy.com](https://drawtonomy.com). Source for the SDK,
extensions, and this documentation site is on
[GitHub](https://github.com/kosuke55/drawtonomy).

## Who this is for

- **Autonomous-driving engineers** building or reviewing scenarios for
  simulators such as [esmini](https://github.com/esmini/esmini),
  CARLA, or in-house tooling.
- **Traffic planners and researchers** sketching intersections, paths, and
  road networks without firing up a heavyweight CAD tool.
- **Driving instructors and educators** producing diagrams for course
  material.
- **Tool builders** who want to extend the editor with new exporters,
  importers, or AI-assisted features through the
  [extension SDK](/extend/).
