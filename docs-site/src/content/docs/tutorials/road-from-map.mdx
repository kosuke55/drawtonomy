In this lesson you start from a real-world location, drop in a
satellite background, and turn the road network into editable
drawtonomy lanes.

<Demo src="/videos/map-to-lane-demo.mp4" caption="Click for one lane, drag for many." maxWidth="900px" />

## 1. Switch on the satellite layer

1. Open the canvas background menu (top-left of the canvas).
2. Pick **Satellite** (or **Roadmap** if you prefer schematic colours).
3. Pan and zoom to the location you want to model.

## 2. Click a road to generate a single lane

1. Press <kbd>N</kbd> for the **Lane** tool, then enable **Generator**
   mode in the toolbar.
2. Hover a road. The Lane Generator highlights the road segment under
   your cursor.
3. Click. drawtonomy fetches OSM data for that segment and converts it
   to a Lane shape with realistic geometry.

## 3. Drag a region to generate many lanes

1. Drag a rectangle on the map to enclose the area you want to capture.
2. Release. Every road inside the rectangle becomes a Lane shape, all
   aligned to the underlying map.

## 4. Tidy up

Generated lanes are normal Lane shapes — you can:

- Drag them, edit their boundaries with [Segment Editing](/guides/segment-editing/),
- Connect them with the Attribute Panel,
- Or [smooth their boundaries](/guides/smooth-lanes/).

<Demo src="/videos/smooth-lane.mp4" caption="Smooth a generated lane to replace dense polylines with splines." />

## 5. Save

Export as `drawtonomy.svg` to keep the satellite alignment and lane
connection info, or as PNG/SVG for sharing.

## What you learned

- The Lane Generator turns OSM roads into editable lanes.
- Drag-to-region is the fastest way to capture a network.
- Generated lanes behave like any other lane and can be refined
  further.

For the full set of generator options, see
[Generate lanes from a map](/guides/lane-from-map/).
