## What is drawtonomy?

drawtonomy is a **whiteboard for driving scenarios** — the
figures you put in a paper, the slide you sketch before a design
review, the diagram you draw on a call when you're trying to
explain a corner case to the rest of the team, the scene you
sketch out before writing the OpenSCENARIO file.

Open the URL, draw lanes and intersections on an infinite canvas,
drop in vehicles, pedestrians, traffic lights, crosswalks, and
road markings, then save or export. Nothing to install, no
account, nothing uploaded.

The reason it exists: generic drawing tools and slide decks don't
know what a lane is, so the road geometry has to be rebuilt every
time something moves. drawtonomy treats lanes, intersections, and
the rest of the driving-scenario vocabulary as built-in shapes,
so the figure stays correct as you iterate.

## Why it's faster than a generic drawing tool

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Driving-domain shapes" icon="seti:default">
		Lanes, intersections, crosswalks, traffic lights, road
		markings, vehicles, and pedestrians come built in. You can
		also add your own SVG templates and contribute them by PR.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Topology-aware lanes" icon="seti:graphql">
		Each lane carries Next / Previous / Left / Right
		connections. Two lanes that share a boundary share the same
		boundary points, so dragging once moves both.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Infinite canvas, satellite backgrounds" icon="seti:html">
		Pan and zoom across arbitrarily large layouts. Drop in a
		satellite or roadmap background and trace from a real
		location. Snap and shared points keep geometry aligned
		without manual cleanup.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Free and browser-only" icon="rocket">
		No install, no account, no upload. Reopen a saved
		drawtonomy.svg later and the scene comes back exactly as
		you left it, connections and overlap relationships
		included.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

## Bridges to your simulation and mapping stack

The whiteboard is the main thing; these are how the diagram flows
out to the rest of the workflow.

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Re-editable SVG for papers and slides" icon="document">
		`drawtonomy.svg` is a regular SVG that previews in
		browsers, GitHub, and slide decks, and reopens in
		drawtonomy with every connection intact. The default for
		figures in documentation and research papers.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Lanelet2 round-trip" icon="seti:json">
		Open Lanelet2 OSM maps for editing and export back to OSM
		— including Autoware sample maps. Useful for sketching
		changes against an existing HD map.
	</Card>
	<Card title="ASAM OpenDRIVE / OpenSCENARIO" icon="seti:db">
		Export OpenDRIVE 1.8 and OpenSCENARIO 1.3 — together as an
		[esmini](https://github.com/esmini/esmini)-ready zip when
		you want to play your scene back in a simulator.
	</Card>
	<Card title="AI Scene Generator" icon="comment-alt">
		Describe a scenario in natural language, or paste
		OpenSCENARIO XML, and get an editable canvas back. The
		output is a normal drawtonomy scene; refine it like
		anything else.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
