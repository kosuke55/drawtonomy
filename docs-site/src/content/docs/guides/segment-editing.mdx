Segment editing lets you tweak shapes between any two adjacent
vertices, add a new vertex, or delete one — without redrawing the
shape from scratch.

<Demo src="/videos/segment-demo.mp4" caption="Double-click to enter segment edit mode, then drag a point or click a segment to add one." />

## Enter segment-editing mode

1. Press <kbd>V</kbd> for the Select tool.
2. Double-click a Linestring, Lane, or Polygon.
3. The shape switches to point-edit mode: each vertex becomes a
   draggable indigo handle.

## Move a point

Click and drag a vertex. If the point is shared with another shape
(see [Point Sharing](/guides/point-sharing/)), every shape that
holds it moves together.

For lanes, dragging a boundary point updates both lanes that share
the boundary — see
[Lane connection model](/explanation/lane-model/).

## Add a point

Click anywhere on a segment (the line between two existing points).
A new vertex is inserted at the click position.

This is the fastest way to introduce a curve into a previously
straight section: add a point, then drag it.

## Remove a point

Select the vertex (it turns solid indigo) and press
<kbd>Delete</kbd> or <kbd>Backspace</kbd>.

## Exit segment editing

Press <kbd>Esc</kbd>, click outside the shape, or pick another
tool. The shape returns to normal selection mode.
