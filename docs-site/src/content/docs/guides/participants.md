---
title: Add vehicles, pedestrians, and traffic lights
description: Place actors and traffic elements on the canvas using built-in templates.
---

drawtonomy ships templates for the actors and traffic elements you need
in driving scenarios.

## Vehicles

1. Press <kbd>P</kbd> to open **Participants**.
2. Pick a vehicle template (Sedan, Bus, Truck, Motorcycle).
3. Click on the canvas to place. The vehicle drops at its template size.

Drag a corner handle to resize. Drag the rotation handle to face it along
a lane.

## Pedestrians

In the same Participants menu, pick a pedestrian template (Walking,
Simple).

## Traffic lights

The Participants menu also includes vehicle and pedestrian signals. Place
them at intersection corners — they are static shapes; they do not run a
signal phase.

## Custom templates

You can add your own SVG templates and contribute them by PR. See the
[Template Guide](https://github.com/kosuke55/drawtonomy/blob/main/templates/TEMPLATE_GUIDE.md).

## Populating a path with vehicles

To place a row of vehicles spaced along a path (for headway diagrams or
follow-the-leader scenes), use [Path Footprint](/guides/path-footprint/).
