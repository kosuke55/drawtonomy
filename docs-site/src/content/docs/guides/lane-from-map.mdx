The Lane Generator pulls real road geometry from OpenStreetMap and
turns it into editable Lane shapes aligned to the map background.

<Demo src="/videos/map-to-lane-demo.mp4" caption="Click a road for one lane, drag a region for many." maxWidth="900px" />

## Switch on a map background

1. Open the **Background** menu in the top-left of the canvas.
2. Pick **Satellite** (photographic) or **Roadmap** (schematic).
3. Pan and zoom to the area you want to model.

The map renders behind the canvas and stays anchored as you draw.

## Generate a single lane

1. Press <kbd>N</kbd> for the Lane tool.
2. Enable **Generator** mode in the toolbar.
3. Hover a road. drawtonomy highlights the matched OSM road segment
   under your cursor.
4. Click. The segment is fetched and converted into a Lane shape.

## Generate every road in a region

With Generator mode active:

1. Drag a rectangle over the area you want to capture.
2. Release. Every road inside the rectangle is converted in a single
   batch.

Use this for whole intersections, neighbourhoods, or test sites.

## Recommended limits

The OSM fetch and conversion runs in the browser. For responsive
editing, keep the resulting scene under roughly **500 lanes** — split a
large area into multiple files if you need more.

## After generation

Generated lanes are normal Lane shapes. You can:

- Drag points to refine geometry.
- Apply [Smooth](/guides/smooth-lanes/) to replace dense polylines with
  splines.
- Set [connections](/guides/lane-connections/) where the generator did
  not infer them automatically.
- Drop vehicles, pedestrians, or paths on top.
