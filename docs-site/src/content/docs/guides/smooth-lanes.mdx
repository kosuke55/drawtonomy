Lanes drawn click-by-click have polyline boundaries, which look angular.
Smoothing replaces them with spline-fitted curves in one click; the
centerline is preserved so connections and length are unchanged.

<Demo src="/videos/smooth-lane.mp4" caption="One click on Smooth in the Attribute Panel." />

## Smooth a single lane

1. Press <kbd>V</kbd> for the Select tool.
2. Click the lane.
3. In the **Attribute Panel** on the right, click **Smooth**.

The boundaries are recomputed in place. The change is a single undo
step — press <kbd>Cmd/Ctrl+Z</kbd> to revert.

## Smooth multiple lanes

1. Drag a selection box around the lanes (or <kbd>Shift</kbd>-click
   each one).
2. Click **Smooth** in the Attribute Panel. The action applies to every
   selected lane in one undo step.

## When smoothing is the right move

- After you draw a curved approach with the [Lane tool](/guides/lane-tool/).
- After [Lane Generator](/guides/lane-from-map/) imports OSM roads,
  which often arrive as dense polylines.
- After [segment editing](/guides/segment-editing/) when you have added
  many intermediate points by hand.

## What smoothing does not change

- The lane's centerline geometry.
- The lane's length, width, or [connections](/guides/lane-connections/).
- Shared boundary points with neighbouring lanes — they stay shared.

If you smooth a lane whose neighbour shares a boundary, the neighbour's
side of the boundary moves with it. This is the correct behaviour for
adjacent lanes; if you want to smooth one side only, ungroup the
neighbour first.
