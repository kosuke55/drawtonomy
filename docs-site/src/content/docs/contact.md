## Bug reports, feature requests, and questions

➡ **[Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/kosuke55/drawtonomy/issues/new)**

A short repro (steps + expected vs. actual) helps a lot for bugs.
Screenshots or a saved `drawtonomy.svg` of the broken state are
also welcome.
